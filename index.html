TreadPace Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 May 2026

TreadPace is designed to be simple and private. The app does not collect personal data.

Data Collection

TreadPace does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal data.

Local Storage

TreadPace stores saved treadmill sessions and app settings locally on your device using local app storage. This information stays on your device and is not sent to the developer, a server, an analytics service, an advertising network, or any third party.

Accounts and Tracking

TreadPace does not use accounts, advertising, tracking, analytics, cloud sync, or a backend.

Deleting Data

You can clear saved sessions inside the app. If you delete the app from your device, locally stored app data may also be deleted.
